# Contacting support

Contact support when:
- something does not work as described in this help, or an error keeps coming back after a reload;
- you need something only Qorva can do, such as deleting your company account, a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), or a billing account problem ("No payment account found", "Failed to set up your billing account");
- the Usage page keeps saying no usage period is active;
- you want to report a security issue.

## How to contact support
1. In this help panel, click **Contact support**.
2. Describe the problem and send the request.
3. You get a reference number straight away, and a reply by email.

## What to include
- What you were trying to do, and the page you were on (for example Match Reports or Account Settings → Integrations).
- The exact error message you saw.
- When it happened, and whether it happens every time.
- The names of the affected job, candidate or integration, if any.
- What you already tried.
Do not include passwords, API keys, tokens or other secrets.
