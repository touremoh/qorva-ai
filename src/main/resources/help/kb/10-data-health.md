# Data Health

**Data Health** gives your Resume Library a health score and lists what to fix. The badge on the menu item shows how many issues are open. If your library is empty, upload resumes first.

## The score and its four checks
The **Library Health** score is rated Excellent, Good, Fair or Needs attention, and tells you which check drags it down.
- **Completeness** — contact details and profile data present on your resumes. **Field Completeness** shows, per field, how many resumes have it, grouped as Critical (email, phone, name, role), Important (work experience, key skills, career start year, education) and Enrichment (languages, certifications, salary expectation, LinkedIn, summary).
- **Freshness** — how current the resume content actually is (based on the dates inside the resume, not the upload date). **Content Freshness** groups resumes as Up to date, Review suggested, Outdated (over 18 months old) or Unknown (no dateable content).
- **Uniqueness** — how many duplicate groups were detected.
- **AI Confidence** — how many resumes the AI could not analyse with confidence and need review.

## AI summary
Click **Show** on **AI summary** for a short written summary of your library's health and "What to do next". It is generated by AI from the numbers on the page — check before acting.

## Issues To Fix
Issues are listed with a severity (Critical, High, Medium): no contact information, missing email, missing phone, no work experience extracted, no skills extracted, missing profile summary, content outdated, freshness unknown, low AI analysis confidence, duplicate resumes. Click **View** to see the affected resumes. From there you can:
- open a resume and **Add** the missing information;
- **Confirm current (N)** — mark selected resumes you verified as still accurate;
- **Archive all** — archive the affected resumes (archived resumes are excluded from matching and quality reporting; unarchive them from the Resume Library at any time);
- **Dismiss — accepted, hide from open issues** — hide an issue you accept. Dismissed issues are listed under **Dismissed (N)** and can be reopened with **Reopen this issue**;
- for duplicates, compare the copies (with the date each was added) and **Delete resume** for the extra ones.

## Re-analyze with AI
**Re-analyze all** re-runs AI extraction on the affected resumes, for example after low-confidence results. The confirmation shows how many screening actions it uses and how many remain this period. Older resumes without stored source text are skipped — re-upload them to refresh. Progress is shown while it runs and you can **Cancel**. The result shows how many were updated, failed or skipped.

## Asking candidates to update their profile
Candidates can refresh their own data.
1. On an issue (for example Outdated), click **Request updates**.
2. Pick an **Email template**, or keep the **Standard Qorva message**. **Manage…** opens Configuration → Email Templates.
3. Click **Send requests**.
Each candidate gets an email with a secure link. On that page they confirm their availability, available-from date, notice period and salary expectations (min, max, currency), and can upload a newer resume (.pdf or .docx). Their profile in Qorva is updated automatically. Candidates without an email address, those who unsubscribed and those with a pending request are skipped automatically. Candidates can unsubscribe from update requests from the email. A link stops working once used or expired.
