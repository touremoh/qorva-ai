# Getting started with Qorva

Qorva is an AI resume-screening and talent-intelligence app for recruiting teams. It reads your resumes (CVs), turns each one into a structured candidate profile, scores candidates against your jobs with scoring rules you define, and explains every score. You can use Qorva on its own by uploading PDF or Word (.docx) files, or connect your applicant tracking system (ATS) to import candidates and jobs automatically. An ATS is not required.

## The main menu
The left menu can be collapsed or expanded. Some items only appear if you have the matching permission.
- **Dashboard** — key numbers (Resumes, Job Posts, Users, Match Reports), Top 10 Skills, Reports per Job, Top 5 Candidates per Job Post, Talent Pool Insight charts, the Candidate pipeline summary (including median time to shortlist) and a usage summary.
- **Resume Library** — all candidate profiles. Upload resumes, search, filter, tag, add notes, email candidates.
- **Data Health** — a health score for your library (completeness, freshness, duplicates, AI confidence) with fixes. The badge shows how many issues are open.
- **Jobs** — your job posts and their scoring rules (matching criteria).
- **Match Reports** — the scored results of matching candidates against jobs. You start matching here with **Run Matching**.
- **Pipeline** — a board that shows where each matched candidate stands (New, Contacted, Shortlisted, and so on).
- **Copilot** — an AI assistant that answers questions about candidates and your library and carries out tasks. Has the tabs Chat, Activity and Rules. Only shown if you have the "Use Copilot" permission.
- **Usage** — how much of your plan's allowance you have used this billing period.
- **Configuration** — company-wide settings such as **Email Templates**.
- **Account Settings** — also opened from the avatar menu (top right). Tabs: **My Profile**, **Company**, **Users**, **Integrations**, **Billing**. The avatar menu also has **Sign Out**.

## A typical first week
1. **Add candidates.** Open **Resume Library** and click **Bulk Upload Resumes** to upload .pdf or .docx files, or connect your ATS in **Account Settings → Integrations** to import them.
2. **Check your data.** Open **Data Health** to see missing contact details, outdated resumes and duplicates.
3. **Create a job.** Open **Jobs**, click **New Job Post**, write or generate the description, then review the AI-suggested **Matching Criteria** (skills, experience, location, industry, weights).
4. **Run matching.** Open **Match Reports**, click **Run Matching**, pick the jobs and how many top candidates you want per job.
5. **Review match reports.** Each report shows the score, strengths, weaknesses, red flags and a recommendation. Ask Copilot follow-up questions about a candidate.
6. **Move candidates along.** Use **Pipeline** to drag candidates from New to Contacted, Shortlisted, Interviewing and so on.
7. **Email candidates.** Use **Email candidate** from a resume or report. Connect a Microsoft 365 mailbox in **My Profile** to send directly from Qorva.
8. **Automate.** Ask Copilot to tag, shortlist or draft emails, or set up **Copilot → Rules** so tasks start by themselves.

## Changing the interface language
Qorva is available in 7 languages: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Português, Italiano and Nederlands.
1. Open **Account Settings → My Profile** (avatar menu, top right).
2. Use the language selector in the profile banner at the top.
The choice applies immediately and is remembered in this browser. You can also change the language on the sign-in page.
