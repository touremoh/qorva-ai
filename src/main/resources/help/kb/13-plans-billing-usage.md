# Plans, billing and usage

## Plans
Each plan includes a number of users, ATS integrations, files per upload, email templates and an allowance of AI work per billing period. Exact numbers depend on your plan — see the plan limits section. Plans can be billed monthly or yearly. The plan cards show a 14-day free trial; current trial and plan terms are on the pricing page and in the Billing tab.

## Billing tab
Open **Account Settings → Billing** (avatar menu, top right) and click **Open Billing Portal**. The secure billing portal (provided by Stripe) lets you view and download invoices, update your plan and change your payment method. You need the **Update Subscription** permission; otherwise you see "Billing is managed by an account owner. Ask them to change the plan or payment details." Cancelling needs the **Cancel Subscription** permission.

**Account Settings → Company** shows your **Subscription**: plan, billing cycle, price, current period start, renewal date and status. Possible statuses: Free trial — active, Active subscription, Payment failed, Expired, Locked, Incomplete setup, Subscription failed, Cancelled, Cancellation scheduled — grace period, Cancelled — grace period ended.

## Usage page
Open **Usage** in the left menu. The header shows your plan, whether it is billed monthly or yearly (with yearly billing, limits cover the whole year), the current period dates and days left, and **Manage plan**. Each meter shows how much you used, a percentage, an all-time total, and a pace indicator: On track, Close to the limit at this pace, "At this pace, runs out ~date", or "Limit reached — resets date". Click **How is this counted?** on a meter for details. **AI summary** gives a written summary and next steps (generated by AI from the numbers — check before acting).

## What each meter counts
- **Matching Actions** (also called screening actions in the app) — one per resume analysed, and one per candidate scored against a job. Counted when a resume is uploaded or imported from your ATS, when a matching run scores a candidate against an open job (only new candidates and changed resumes or jobs — unchanged reports are reused for free — up to the Top N you choose per job), when Data Health re-analyses a resume, and when AI suggests scoring rules. At the limit, uploads, ATS imports, matching and re-analysis pause until the period renews.
- **Copilot · candidate questions** — one per Copilot answer about one candidate (from the full resume, the job and its match report). At the limit, Copilot can't answer questions about a candidate until the period renews.
- **Copilot · library analyses** — one per Copilot analysis of your whole library (counts, charts or candidate cards), follow-ups included. At the limit, Copilot can't analyse the library until the period renews.
- **Copilot · tasks** — one per other Copilot request: searching, tagging, drafting, matching, rules — whether you gave it in chat or a rule started it. At the limit, new tasks are refused until the period renews; questions still work, and rules pause.
- **Email templates** — saved invitation templates. A plan allowance, not reset each period.

If the Usage page says "No usage period is active for this account", a period normally opens a few minutes after a subscription starts or renews. If it persists, contact support.
