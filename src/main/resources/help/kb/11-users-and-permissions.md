# Users and permissions

Manage your team in **Account Settings → Users** (avatar menu, top right). You need the **Manage Users** permission to add users, change permissions or remove users; **View Users** only lets you see the list.

## Inviting a user
1. Click **Add User**.
2. Enter First Name, Last Name and Email (their work email).
3. Choose the role: **Manager** or **Owner**.
4. Set their **Access Permissions** (see below).
5. Click **Add User**.
Qorva emails them an invite link. They choose their own password from the link, which is valid for 3 days. Until they do, the list shows **Invite pending**. If the link expired or got lost, click **Re-send invite**; a new link is sent and the previous one stops working.

If your company requires Microsoft sign-in, invited users sign in with their company Microsoft account using the same email as in Qorva.

## Seats
The number of users depends on your plan (see the plan limits section). When all seats are used, you see "Seat limit reached for your plan. Please upgrade to add more users." Remove a user or change your plan in **Billing**.

## Editing permissions
Click **Manage Permissions** on a user, switch permissions on or off, and click **Save Changes**. Permissions are grouped as follows (UI labels):
- **Dashboard** — View Dashboard.
- **Resumes** — Add Resume, View Resumes, Edit Resumes, Delete Resumes.
- **Job Posts** — Add Job Post, View Job Posts, Edit Job Posts, Delete Job Posts.
- **Reports** — Generate Reports (run matching), View Reports, Edit Reports (also needed to move candidates on the Pipeline), Delete Reports.
- **User Management** — View Users, Manage Users.
- **ATS Export** — ATS Export.
- **ATS Integrations** — Manage ATS integrations.
- **Candidate outreach** — Email candidates.
- **Copilot** — Use Copilot.
- **Billing** — Update Subscription, Cancel Subscription.

Without a permission, the related buttons or menu items are hidden. Users who can manage users also see the team's Copilot activity and rules, and can edit the Company tab.

## Removing a user
Click **Delete** on the user and confirm. This cannot be undone. You cannot delete yourself. Copilot rules owned by a removed user are paused.
